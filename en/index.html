Research competitors and keywords, classify by Search Intent, plus keyword gap analysis using Ahrefs, Keyword Planner, Keywordtool.io and more.

02

Planning

Build 6-12 month roadmaps, set KPIs aligned with Traffic, Rankings and Revenue, prioritize URLs based on objectives and available team resources.

03

Technical

Run regular website audits using Ahrefs, Screaming Frog, GSC and GA4. Review Schema, Indexation, Sitemaps, robots.txt, On-page and Off-page.

04

Develop guidelines and checklists for each team, propose outlines, manage internal links and optimize Category, Product and Blog pages.

05

Reporting - Optimize

06
